Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

  • Your Google account email and name, through Google Sign-In.
  • A device identifier, used to register your account to your device.
  • Usage-time counters used to apply free-plan limits.
  • Your subscription status and expiry date.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. STREC never sees or stores your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to payment data.

Where data is stored

Account and subscription data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase.

Live streaming

If you use live streaming, your stream goes directly from your device to the destination you choose, such as YouTube Live. Your stream key stays on your device.

Advertising

The free version may show ads from third-party ad networks [CONFIRM]. These providers may collect data under their own policies.

Your recordings

Recordings are stored on your device. They are not uploaded unless you choose to use a cloud feature.

Permissions

STREC may ask for permissions such as screen capture, microphone, notifications and display over other apps. Each is used only for the feature it relates to.

How we use data

To sign you in, apply plan limits, activate PRO after payment, prevent misuse and provide support. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. To request deletion, email strecapphelp@gmail.com from your registered Google email.

Children

STREC is not directed to young children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
