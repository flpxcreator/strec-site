About

STREC is built by an independent developer in India.

What STREC is

STREC is an Android app for screen recording and live streaming. It is made to be simple to start, and to give you more control over quality when you need it.

Why it exists

Recording a phone screen should not mean fighting with settings or settling for blurry video. STREC aims to make high-quality recording and streaming approachable for everyone, from gamers to teachers.

Free and PRO

You can start for free with 1080p Smooth (60 FPS) and 720p Ultra-Light. STREC PRO unlocks higher resolutions and frame rates, filters, audio and more, as a simple one-time purchase.
