Screen recording

Record your way.

1. Quality & Resolution

Choose the mode that fits your game and your phone.

Auto (Match Display Hz) PRO
Auto-matches your display: 120 FPS on a 120 Hz screen, 60 FPS on a 60 Hz screen.
1080p Esports (120 FPS) PRO
Ultra-smooth 120 FPS for BGMI and other high-refresh gaming.
1080p Smooth (60 FPS)
1080p target at 60 FPS and 16 Mbps.
720p Ultra-Light
720p target, built for zero lag. Works with all filters.
4K High-Res (60 FPS) PRO
4K at 60 FPS for maximum flagship clarity.

2. Audio Sources

Decide exactly what gets recorded.

Internal Audio Only
Captures device and game playback audio through Android AudioPlaybackCapture. No microphone.
Internal Audio + Microphone
Captures playback audio and your microphone at the same time.
Microphone Only
Captures microphone audio only.

3. Filters

Active GPU shaders with simple sliders.

Hue
Shift the colour tone of the whole recording.
Saturation
Make colours richer or calmer.
Vibrance
Boost muted colours without over-saturating the rest.
Clarity
Add punch to mid-tone detail.
Structure
Sharpen fine texture and edges.
Reset Filters
Return every slider to its default in one tap.

4. Controls & Comfort

Settings that keep recording smooth and safe.

Screen Safe Area
Proportionally adjust UI scaling to prevent screen overflow and fit your display perfectly. UI scale from 75% (compact) to 120% (enlarged).
Advanced Microphone Permission
Lets your voice be recorded in your video or stream while still reaching teammates in the game's voice chat. Needs a special Android permission.
Background Power
Keeps recording smooth in the background and protects against thermal throttling and background app kills, even when your phone gets warm during high-FPS gameplay.
STREC LIVE

Go live from your phone.

A hardware-accelerated RTMP stream engine, designed to stream your screen with quality and control.

Live Stream Quality

Choose resolution and bitrate for your network.

720p 60 FPS
5 Mbps. Needs an upload speed of about 5 to 10 Mbps.
1080p 60 FPS
15 Mbps. Needs an upload speed of about 15 to 20 Mbps.
4K 60 FPS PRO
25 Mbps. The app recommends a connection of at least 65 Mbps for better quality.

Live streaming tools

Everything around the stream.

Hardware-accelerated RTMP engine
A dedicated live stream engine built for smooth, efficient streaming.
Aspect Ratio & Screen Layout
Landscape (16:9, 1920×1080) for YouTube streams, or Vertical (9:16, 1080×1920) for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.
Virtual Screen Adjuster
Adjust zoom and overlays for the stream canvas.
Broadcast Destination
Stream to YouTube Live with your RTMP ingest server URL and private stream key.
Live Audio & Mixing
Real-time dual-track gain and a smart voice gate. Choose Internal, Internal + Mic or Mic Only, and set the game audio level.
Speed Test & Quality Advisor
Test your real upload speed before going live. STREC recommends the best quality for your connection: 720p, 1080p or 4K.
Floating In-Game Bubble
Optional. Needs the "Display over other apps" permission. With it off you get a clean stream with no floating icon, and you can stop from the notification panel.
Stream Diagnostics & Crash Logs
View stream diagnostics and logs to understand what happened during a session.
